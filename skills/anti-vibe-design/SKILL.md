# Anti-Vibe-Coding & Anti-AI-Slop Web Design Guidelines

## Core Directive
Never build websites that look "vibe-coded" or generated by amateur AI prompts. Eliminate all dead giveaways of lazy AI generation, artificial social proof, and disorienting UI gimmicks. Build clean, trustworthy, high-craft production web applications.

---

## 🚫 The 12 AI-Slop Anti-Patterns (NEVER DO)

### 1. ❌ No Purple Gradients
* **The Slop:** Purple-to-blue or violet-to-pink gradient text, gradient borders, and glowing purple mesh backgrounds (`bg-gradient-to-r from-purple-500 to-indigo-600`). This is the universal hallmark of generic AI templates.
* **The Standard:** Use intentional, brand-specific color palettes. Rely on solid, high-contrast, purposeful colors with neutral backgrounds (deep blacks, clean slates, warm zincs, or crisp whites) and restrained single-accent highlights.

### 2. ❌ No Pill-Shaped Buttons
* **The Slop:** `rounded-full` buttons everywhere on landing pages, search bars, and CTAs.
* **The Standard:** Use modern, structured geometry (`rounded-md` or `rounded-lg`, 6px to 10px corner radius). Reserve rounded-full strictly for tags, badges, and avatars.

### 3. ❌ No Fake Reviews & Testimonials
* **The Slop:** "John D., CEO of TechCorp: 'This tool changed our entire company!'" paired with Unsplash headshots, AI avatars, or 5 glowing yellow stars with no links.
* **The Standard:** If you don't have real customer reviews yet, **do not fake them**. Show real product screenshots, interactive demos, benchmark data, or GitHub stars instead. Real traction beats fabricated praise 100% of the time.

### 4. ❌ No Fake Metrics
* **The Slop:** Arbitrary statistics in big bold numbers: "99.9% Efficiency", "10x Productivity Boost", "100M+ Requests Processed" with zero verifiable source or audit.
* **The Standard:** Only display real, verifiable data. If reporting benchmarks, cite the test environment, latency measurements, or methodology.

### 5. ❌ No Vague Hero Text
* **The Slop:** "Supercharge your workflow with next-generation AI synergy", "Unlock the future of collaborative productivity", "Empower your team with intelligent orchestration".
* **The Standard:** Write direct, concrete value propositions that explain **what the product actually does in the first sentence**. E.g.: "Open-source context compaction for AI coding agents" or "PostgreSQL schema migrations with automatic rollback".

### 6. ❌ No Emoji Icons
* **The Slop:** Using emojis as feature list bullets, category icons, or section headers (🚀 Fast, 🔒 Secure, 💡 Smart, ⚡ Lightning).
* **The Standard:** Always use professional, crisp vector icon libraries (Lucide Icons, Heroicons, Radix Icons, Feather).

### 7. ❌ No Em Dashes in Copy
* **The Slop:** Relentless overuse of em dashes ("Our platform—designed from the ground up—empowers teams to scale—effortlessly."). This is an immediate fingerprint of unedited LLM generation.
* **The Standard:** Write crisp, active, punctuated sentences. Use periods, commas, or parentheses naturally.

### 8. ❌ No Crazy Scroll Animations
* **The Slop:** Aggressive scroll-jacking, elements spinning into view from off-screen, sections locking until you scroll 5 times, or elements rotating 360 degrees on viewport entry.
* **The Standard:** Subtle, performant, standard reveals (opacity 0 → 1, 10px translate-y fade, standard 200ms ease-out) or native CSS transitions. Content should always be accessible immediately without waiting for scroll triggers.

### 9. ❌ No AI Slop Photos & 3D Clay Avatars
* **The Slop:** Glossy 3D clay characters floating with laptops, uncanny valley smiling synthetic faces, or generic neon cyberpunk brain illustrations.
* **The Standard:** Real product UI screenshots, code snippets with syntax highlighting, architectural diagrams, or authentic photography.

### 10. ❌ No AI Slop Marketing Copy
* **The Slop:** "In today's fast-paced digital landscape, staying ahead of the curve is more crucial than ever before. Delve into a seamless tapestry of innovation."
* **The Standard:** Clear, honest, concise human prose. Cut adverbs, cut filler, explain the features, pricing, and how to get started.

### 11. ❌ No Cursor Animations
* **The Slop:** Custom laggy trailing dots, trailing sparkles, cursor circle enlarge-on-hover, or replacing the native operating system cursor with a customized canvas canvas follower.
* **The Standard:** Keep the native system cursor (`cursor-pointer`, `cursor-default`). Never hijack mouse movement.

### 12. ❌ No Fake Customer Counters / Social Proof Popups
* **The Slop:** "10,249 happy founders using our app", or fake bottom-left toast notifications: "Sarah from London just purchased the Pro plan 2 minutes ago!"
* **The Standard:** Completely ban fake activity tickers and fake purchase toasts. They destroy credibility instantly.

## ✅ The 5 Production Essentials (MUST HAVE)

Every web application and landing page shipped must include these 5 critical elements:

### 1. ✅ Custom Domain
* Ensure the project is configured for a proper custom domain (`app.yourdomain.com` or `yourproject.dev`), not an ugly default staging subdomain (`sparkling-unicorn-1234.vercel.app` or `my-test-proj.web.app`).

### 2. ✅ Proper Favicon System
* Never leave the default framework favicon (Next.js triangle, Vite logo, React atom).
* Provide:
  * `/favicon.ico` (multi-res 16x16, 32x32)
  * `/icon.svg` (crisp vector)
  * `/apple-touch-icon.png` (180x180)
  * Proper `<link rel="icon">` and OpenGraph metadata (`og:image`, `twitter:card`).

### 3. ✅ Remove All "Made with AI" Tags
* Audit and strip all default builder badges, "Built with v0", "Made with Lovable", "Generated by Bolt", or similar watermarks from the footer, header, and metadata.

### 4. ✅ Dedicated Privacy Policy Page (`/privacy`)
* Provide a real, comprehensive, accessible Privacy Policy page detailing data collection, cookies, authentication storage, and contact information.

### 5. ✅ Dedicated Terms & Conditions Page (`/terms`)
* Provide a clear Terms of Service / Terms & Conditions page specifying service usage, billing/refund terms (if applicable), limitations of liability, and governing law.
