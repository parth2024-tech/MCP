# Investor Pitch Deck Blueprint

**Deck type:** Investor / Pitch Deck
**Narrative model:** Problem → Why Now → Solution → Market → Traction → Team → Ask
**Target count:** 13 slides + appendix
**Delivery:** Live (10–15 min) + PDF leave-behind

---

## Slide Blueprint

| # | Type | Archetype | One-sentence message |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Title | Title Slide | \[Company\] is \[what you do in 10 words or less\] |
| 2 | Problem | Statement + Data | \[X%\] of \[target persona\] suffers from \[specific pain\] today |
| 3 | Why Now | Statement + Diagram | Three forces converging in \[year\] make this the right moment |
| 4 | Solution | Product + Screenshot | \[Company\] \[specific mechanic\] eliminates \[specific pain\] in \[timeframe\] |
| 5 | Market Size | Data (3-tier chart) | A \$\[X\]B market is ours to capture; we are entering through the \[SAM\] |
| 6 | Product | Full-bleed screenshot | \[Key workflow\] takes \[X seconds\] vs. \[Y hours\] today |
| 7 | Business Model | Table/Tier slide | We earn \$\[X\] per \[unit\] on a \[subscription/transaction/usage\] model |
| 8 | Traction | KPI Dashboard | \[MRR/ARR\], \[users\], \[retention\] prove this works |
| 9 | Go-to-Market | Process flowchart | We acquire the first \[N\] customers through \[channel\] then expand via \[channel\] |
| 10 | Competition | 2×2 Matrix | We uniquely combine \[X\] and \[Y\] — no competitor does both |
| 11 | Team | Team Slide | Three operators who have done this before: \[credentials\] |
| 12 | Financials | Data slide | We reach \[milestone\] by \[date\] with this raise; gross margin is \[X%\] |
| 13 | The Ask | Statement + CTA | We are raising \$\[X\] to achieve \[milestone 1\], \[milestone 2\], \[milestone 3\] |

## Appendix Blueprint

| # | Label | Content |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | Customer Quotes | 2–3 real quotes from paying/pilot customers |
| A2 | Technical Architecture | System diagram (for technical investors) |
| A3 | Unit Economics | CAC, LTV, LTV/CAC ratio, payback period |

## Narrative Notes

### Slide 2 — Problem
- Make the pain visceral and specific. Don't say "the process is slow."
- Say: "A mid-market CFO spends 14 hours per quarter reconciling data that should take 20 minutes."
- Support with a real statistic (source it) or a direct customer quote (name optional, company optional).

### Slide 3 — Why Now
- Three triggers that create the moment: technology shift, regulatory change, behavioral change, market event.
- Not "AI is everywhere" — that's vague. Specific: "GPT-4-class models dropped inference cost 90% in 18 months, making real-time \[X\] viable for the first time."

### Slide 5 — Market Size
- Compute bottom-up, not press-release headlines.
- TAM: total addressable universe × annual value per unit
- SAM: subset you can realistically serve in 3 years
- SOM: what you will realistically capture in 18 months
- Source every number.

### Slide 8 — Traction
- Lead with the metric the audience cares most about (revenue > users > signups)
- Show the trend (MoM growth chart is more compelling than a single number)
- If pre-revenue: LOIs, pilots, waitlist count, or validated user interviews count — label them honestly

### Slide 10 — Competition
- Never say "We have no competitors" — this signals poor market research
- Plot on 2 axes that matter to your buyer (e.g., Ease of integration vs. Depth of analytics)
- You must be in the top-right quadrant. If you're not, revisit your axis labels or your positioning.

### Slide 13 — The Ask
- Be specific: "We are raising \$3M on a SAFE with a \$12M cap"
- Use of funds: 3 buckets max (Headcount 60%, Product 25%, GTM 15%)
- Milestones: what does this money buy you? ("18 months runway to \$1M ARR and Series A readiness")

## Quality Gate Checklist (Before Sending)

```
☐ Every metric is sourced or labeled "(internal data)"
☐ No market size number copy-pasted from a headline without computation
☐ Competition slide does not claim "no competitors"
☐ Team slide has one credential per person, not a full bio
☐ Ask slide states: amount, structure (SAFE/equity/note), and 3 milestones funded
☐ Font ≥ 24pt on all body text
☐ Deck is ≤ 15 slides in the main section
☐ PDF exported and tested on a machine without your fonts
☐ All logos used are from approved brand kits (no scraped low-res PNGs)
```
