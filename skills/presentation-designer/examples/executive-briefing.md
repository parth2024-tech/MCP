# Executive Briefing Blueprint

**Deck type:** Executive Briefing (BLUF — Bottom Line Up Front)
**Narrative model:** Decision → Recommendation → Context → Options → Risk → Ask
**Target count:** 5–8 slides
**Delivery:** 10–15 minute meeting OR async read

---

## Slide Blueprint

| # | Type | Archetype | One-sentence message |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Decision Needed | Statement | We need a decision on \[X\] by \[date\] to achieve \[outcome\] |
| 2 | Recommendation | Single-point | We recommend \[specific option\] because it delivers \[specific outcome\] |
| 3 | Context | Single-point | These three facts explain why we are at this decision point |
| 4 | Options Considered | Comparison table | We evaluated \[N\] options; \[recommended\] wins on \[criteria\] |
| 5 | Impact & Risk | Two-column | Approving delivers \[X\]; not deciding by \[date\] costs \[Y\] |
| 6 | Next Steps | CTA | Three actions, three owners, three dates |

---

## Narrative Notes

### Slide 1 — Decision Needed
Write: "We need a decision on [X] by [date] to [outcome]."
Example: "We need a decision on the AWS vs. Azure migration by November 15 to avoid a $400K renewal on our legacy infrastructure."

### Slide 2 — Recommendation
Lead with the recommendation directly. Do not warm up to it.
- "We recommend Option B: phased migration to AWS over 6 months."
- Three supporting bullets, max 8 words each.

### Slide 3 — Context
Only include the minimum context needed to understand the recommendation. If the audience already knows it, skip this slide.

### Slide 4 — Options Considered
- Show 2–4 options in a comparison table
- Columns: Option name, Key advantage, Key disadvantage, Cost, Timeline
- Highlight the recommended option with accent color column header

### Slide 5 — Impact & Risk
Left column: "If approved" — quantified positive impact
Right column: "If delayed / declined" — quantified cost of inaction
Both columns must have concrete numbers, not vague descriptions.

### Slide 6 — Next Steps
Format: "By [date], [owner] will [specific action]"
Maximum 3 next steps. If there are more, list the top 3 and reference the project plan.

---

## Anti-patterns for Executive Briefings

- ❌ Starting with background before the recommendation
- ❌ Using analysis-heavy slides in the main deck (it belongs in appendix)
- ❌ Vague next steps ("We will discuss further")
- ❌ Slides that require reading to understand the point (the headline must be sufficient)
- ❌ More than 8 slides in the main section
